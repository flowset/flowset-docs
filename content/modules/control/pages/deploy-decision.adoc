= Deploying a Decision Table
:description: Deploying a DMN decision table to the connected engine.

You can deploy a DMN decision table to the connected engine directly from Control. Deployed tables appear in the xref:dmn-decisions.adoc[Decision Tables] view, and the deployment record appears in the xref:deployments.adoc[Deployments] view.

[[deploy]]
== Deploying

To deploy a DMN table:

. In the *Decisions* view, click *Deploy*
. In the *New decision deployment* view, click *Upload* and select your DMN 1.1(.dmn or .dmn11.xml) file.
+
image::deploy-decision.png[align="center", width="1186"]
. Click *OK* to complete deployment.
